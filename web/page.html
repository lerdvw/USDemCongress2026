Make your plan to vote

Every state runs its own elections, so each step below links to your own state’s rules.

How will you vote?

Voting early, in person

  1. Look up your state’s early-voting dates, places and hours at Can I Vote.
  2. Check which ID to bring.
  3. Pick a day and a time, and put it in your calendar.

Voting by mail

  1. Find out how to get a mail ballot in your state, and by when, at Can I Vote. If your state needs a request, send it now.
  2. Follow the ballot’s instructions to the letter, and sign wherever it asks.
  3. Send it back early, in a way your state accepts, so it arrives in time.

Voting on {{ election_day_short }}

  1. Find your polling place at Can I Vote or VOTE411. It may have moved since you last voted.
  2. Check your state’s poll hours and which ID to bring.
  3. Plan when you’ll go, and who you’ll bring with you.
